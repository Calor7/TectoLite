# Strive: geological history design

## Current revision: polygon geology and Euler-motion review

The user's follow-up requested geological polygons in the style of Asterra Rebuilt, consistent Euler motion, additional island groups/accretion, and consideration of every basic event family in Worldbuilding Pasta. The current deliverable is `output/STrive_Rebuilt/STrive_Rebuilt_1200Myr.json`; the earlier 900 Myr version below is preserved separately.

The revision has no feature icons. It contains twelve genuinely rigid shield polygons, retained crust after Kestrel arc and Ember plateau accretion, six native fusions, coherent island-group rotation histories, and separate active/fossil geological provinces. Continent motion uses composed finite Euler rotations across material ownership changes. The geological clock was stretched to 1,200 Myr to keep motion rates within the selected reconstruction limits without changing the paths. Coast exposure and mobile-margin geometry remain authored deformation, explicitly distinguished from rigid cratons.

The process review is in `output/STrive_Rebuilt/PASTA_EVENT_REVIEW.txt`; it records forty event families and original decisions, including exclusions. The source framework is [Worldbuilding Pasta Part Va](https://worldbuildingpasta.blogspot.com/2020/01/an-apple-pie-from-scratch-part-va.html), the [GPlates supplement](https://worldbuildingpasta.blogspot.com/2020/06/an-apple-pie-from-scratch-part-v.html), [tectonics and volcanism](https://worldbuildingpasta.blogspot.com/2021/07/an-apple-pie-from-scratch-part-viia.html), and [erosion](https://worldbuildingpasta.blogspot.com/2022/02/an-apple-pie-from-scratch-part-viib.html). These inform the design; every world date, rotation, region and event selection is invented. Asterra supplies the editable polygon-layer precedent, not Strive's geological history.

Validation checks the native importer and simulation engine, nonchronological scrubbing, finite spherical coordinates, calendar path preservation, rigid-core area and internal distances, core containment, offshore island overlap, accretion crust retention, coherent island frames, normal velocity at the four main spreading systems, the Cinder opening/compression transition, and relative motion along the western transform. Numerical findings and actual sampling limits are recorded in `VALIDATION.json`. This remains a fantasy kinematic reconstruction with representative oceanic domains, not a global force-balanced simulation or complete oceanic plate circuit.

## Research and application

- [Worldbuilding Pasta, Part Va: Constructing a Plate Tectonic History](https://worldbuildingpasta.blogspot.com/2020/01/an-apple-pie-from-scratch-part-va.html) informed independent regional ocean cycles, inherited continental crust, failed rifts, continental ribbons, and plate reorganization. Applied through the Crown basin opening at 170, reversing at 295, closing at 400, and reopening at 490; independently, the Dawn basin opens at 250, reverses at 395, closes at 540, and reopens at 640. Vesper transfers from the southern heartland to Southreach; Saffron transfers to the northern assembly. Both later detach. These sequences and all names are invented for Strive.
- [Worldbuilding Pasta, Part V supplement: Using GPlates](https://worldbuildingpasta.blogspot.com/2020/06/an-apple-pie-from-scratch-part-v.html) informed persistent cratons, changing plate membership, reconstruction at multiple time slices, and inherited mountain generations. Applied through twelve craton overlays, four actual native fusion episodes, eleven native split events, five-Myr coast stages, and distinct active and eroded collision features. Small continental margin adjustments avoid the major interpenetration produced by unrestricted rigid trajectories. This is authored deformation rather than a calculation of crustal mass or elevation.
- [Worldbuilding Pasta, Part VIIa: Tectonics and Volcanism](https://worldbuildingpasta.blogspot.com/2021/07/an-apple-pie-from-scratch-part-viia.html) informed contrasting surface consequences. Applied through western volcanic shutdown and inland uplift, a later transform interval and renewed volcanism, eastern arc retreat and back-arc inversion, short flood-basalt episodes with persistent remnants, and a moving island trail above a fixed plume. Several small volcanic islands emerge at different times instead of existing throughout the reconstruction.
- [USGS, Valu Fa Ridge back-arc spreading center](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/subalkaline-andesite-valu-fa-ridge-a-back-arc-spreading-center-southern-lau-basin) provides a primary geological example that back-arc spreading centers exist. Accordingly, Cinder rollback includes an explicit spreading line before the later compression phase. The blog is guidance for worldbuilding, not an infallible geological authority.

## Scope and checks

The native save carries the full timeline, source links, chapter descriptions, plate ancestry, timed feature generations, and editable geometry. All fifteen original final landmass IDs, colors, positions, and coast corners are retained. Original continent names remain; numbered fragments receive distinct names.

Validation uses TectoLite's own project parser, rotation model, and simulation engine. It checks finite spherical coordinates, endpoint agreement, and nonchronological scrubbing across split and fusion boundaries. The companion atlases are rendered from the save's geometry. Detailed check results are beside the save.

This is a fantasy reconstruction, not a global force-balanced geodynamic simulation. Ocean polygons and boundaries are representative; automatic ocean generation is disabled. Coast stages include changes in shelf exposure and deformation. The save does not solve bathymetry, climate, erosion rates, crustal thickness, or a complete global ocean plate network. Boundary slivers can remain at interpolated contacts. Users should export **Entire Timeline** when preserving the history.
